A Claude Code plugin for Mac and Windows

Be the director. Showcase and Claude do the rest.

Tell Claude the story you want to tell. Claude writes the script, lays out the scenes and times the captions. Showcase Studio turns it into a video for TikTok, Instagram, YouTube Shorts and the App Store.

1. Tell the story

Say what the video is about

Tell Claude what your app does and who the video is for, and bring your screenshots and screen recordings. Claude writes the script, picks a layout for each scene and times the captions to the words.

The studio plays it back as a finished video beside the chat. No timeline, no video editor.

Your noteMake a one-minute TikTok about the plugin. Show what one script turns into.

2. Give notes

Watch it, then say what to change

"Cut the second scene." "Make the hook punchier." "Use a better voice." Claude makes the change, and the video updates as you watch. To fix a word yourself, edit it right in the studio. Claude sees what you changed.

Your crew runs on the Claude Code subscription you already have, and can use any tool on your machine. Here, Claude makes a new voiceover with the director's own AWS account.

3. Step in front of the camera

A teleprompter on your phone

When the video needs your face, the script scrolls on your phone like a teleprompter while you record.

The studio cuts your take into scenes and keeps your voice in sync with the picture. You don't line anything up by hand.

4. Release it

Post to TikTok, Instagram and YouTube Shorts

The studio exports a full-quality vertical video and writes the post text for each platform, within its limits. It copies the text and opens the upload page. On a Mac, it can AirDrop the video to your phone.

You press Post. Nothing is linked to your accounts.

Download

Showcase Studio is a Claude Code plugin. Install the plugin, and it installs the app the first time you use it. Or download the app yourself.

1. Install the plugin

In a terminal:

claude plugin marketplace add kgilpin/showcase-studio
claude plugin install showcase-studio@showcase-studio

Then start Claude Code in a folder for your showcases and run /showcase-studio:showcase new.

2. Or get the app

Version 0.5.0. On a Mac, unzip it and move it to Applications.
