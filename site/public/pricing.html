Questions

What counts as a full-quality video?
The finished 1080×1920 video, or the 4:3 one for YouTube and the web. Drafts, App Store screenshots and the script never count.
Where's my license key?
If you subscribed in the app, it's already there. Otherwise the page after checkout shows it, and every receipt Stripe emails you carries it. In the app, click Trial at the top right and choose I have a key.
How do I cancel, or change my card?
In the app, open the gear button and choose Manage subscription. Or manage your subscription here: enter the email you paid with, and Stripe sends you a link. You don't need your key.
Do I need anything else?
Claude Code. Claude writes your videos, so Showcase Studio runs on the Claude subscription you already have.
What about tax?
Payments go through Stripe, which adds any sales tax or VAT where you live.
